FIELD CONSTRAINTS

The operational challenge

CONSTRAINT 01

Scarce mobile resources

Fixed pool of 12 high-capacity mobile pumps and 8 field crews must protect over 12 recurring sub-surface vulnerability nodes across 1,483 km² of NCT Delhi.

POOL: 12 PUMPS / 8 CREWS
CONSTRAINT 02

Lead time vs transit time

Underpass sumps fill in 15–40 minutes once drainage headers surcharge, while mobilizing heavy dewatering rigs through pre-storm congestion takes 45–75 minutes.

TRANSIT GAP: Δ 30–35 MIN
CONSTRAINT 03

Post-event regret

Equal-split dispatch strategies disperse pumps too thinly, guaranteeing simultaneous breaches across arterial hospital and rail corridors.

POLICY FAILURE: UNIFORM SPLIT
METHODOLOGY

How it works

Three-step hydrological balance and dispatch simulation

01 / ENVELOPE CONFIG

Define rainfall envelope

Input cumulative rainfall (10–250 mm) or select IMD threshold brackets (Moderate, Heavy, Very Heavy). Alternatively load calibrated historical downpour replays.

INPUT: Q_in = C × I × A
02 / HYDRAULIC SOLVER

Compute culvert breach curves

Rational runoff calculations combined with stage-storage curves estimate exact minutes to alert (15 cm) and complete vehicular closure (20 cm) at each underpass.

OUTPUT: T_alert(15cm) / T_close(20cm)
03 / DISPATCH OPTIMIZER

Optimize pump & crew allocation

Monte Carlo combinatorial testing evaluates dispatch permutations to recommend the exact pump assignment that minimizes total regional closure-hours.

OBJECTIVE: min Σ(Closure_hours × W_corridor)
